Artwaka

A voyaging event series and collaborative platform combining contemporary Oceanic arts with intentional space for kava-talanoa — curated by NDINA Gallery.

Rooted in the shared significance of ‘waka’ — premium noble kava roots in Fijian tradition, and the traditional canoe in Māori culture — Artwaka celebrates Pacific heritage and contemporary art as a living, connected force.

Siam Voyage — Opening Night at HOPE Space Thonburi
